The badge is the point

Three facts decide whether an app stays listed. They are re-checked every day and the old answer is overwritten, not stacked.

Live checked

Live check

A HEAD request goes to the listing URL. Anything other than HTTP 200 turns the card red and drops it from the front page until it answers again.

No signup

No signup

The URL must open the working app. A landing page that asks for an email before showing anything does not qualify.

Free

Free or BYOK

The whole app is free, or free with an optional key you already own. Anything with a paywall is either removed or clearly marked as a paid service.

Timestamps come from the checker run, not from copy. data/apps.json is in the repository, so you can read the same numbers the page shows.
